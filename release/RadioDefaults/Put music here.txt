Birb Cube radio

Put music files in this folder: mp3, wav, flac, m4a, aac, wma or ogg.
Each sub-folder becomes its own station on the dial. Loose files here make one more, called "Radio".
Folder stations keep broadcasting while you listen to something else, so you tune in mid-song.

To name a station and pick its frequency, put a station.txt in its folder:
    name: Emberwing FM
    frequency: 92.3

Live web stations come from Radio Browser: click the radio's dial to find stations and pick genres.
They're kept in my-stations.json here.
The radio looks at this folder again every time you switch it on.
