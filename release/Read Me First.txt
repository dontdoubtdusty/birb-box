Birb Cube - read me first

TO PLAY
1. Unzip the whole folder somewhere you can write to (Desktop or Documents, not Program Files).
2. Open the folder and double-click BirbCube.exe.
Nothing needs installing. Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit only.

IF WINDOWS WARNS YOU
The game is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may say it "protected your PC".
Click "More info", then "Run anyway". If it says VCRUNTIME140.dll is missing, install the
Microsoft Visual C++ Redistributable (x64) from Microsoft, then try again.

YOUR WORLD
Your terrarium is saved next to the game (birbcube-save.json) every couple of minutes and when you quit.
To move to a newer version, copy birbcube-save.json and birbcube-settings.json into the new folder.

THE RADIO
Click the dial on the radio to find web stations (a few are already on the dial).
To play your own music, put files or folders of files in the Radio folder (see "Put music here.txt").

CONTROLS
Click the Birb Cube logo: power on/off      Esc: menu (has a Controls page)
Drag sideways: turn the cube                Drag up/down: tilt
Wheel: zoom                                 Double-click the cube: Hologram View (again: leave)
Hold right mouse: pour seed                 Click a bird: select it     WASD: pan
P or Space: pause                           1 / 2 / 3: speed            F: fullscreen
B: Birb Designer                            F9: screenshot              Tab: tools panel

If it crashes, a crash.txt appears next to the game with the details.
